I analyzed placements, spend, ad clicks and order volume. In October 2026, ad-attributed sales increased by 112.1% and units sold by 177.2% compared with September. Ad intensity and clicks also grew. This is a two-month comparison, not evidence of a continuous annual trend.

Integra Hygiene

I worked with a core campaign and a separate COVID product campaign to analyze seasonality independently. January 2026 delivered the account’s highest monthly volume in the period: 5,733 ad-attributed units. Advertising metrics should not be mistaken for total account revenue.

Scope and methods. Shoper: matched months, April–October 2025/2026, platform order values. Google Ads: June–October campaign attribution. Allegro Ads: November 2025–October 2026, platform-attributed ad sales. Metrics from different systems are not additive and do not demonstrate exclusive personal causation.
